# Dish photos — provenance

Every file here is derived from the restaurant's own studio photography, uploaded to zamanigrill.com (WordPress, May 2015) by the business and recovered on 2026-11-03. Originals: `img/source/own/`. Conversion: center crop to 4:3 where the source is 1280×656, resized to 900px (`name.webp`) and 480px (`name-sm.webp`), WebP q80. Owner retains all rights; no third-party images are used in this folder.
